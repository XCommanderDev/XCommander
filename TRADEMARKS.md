# XCommander name and logo

The XCommander source code is free software under the [GPLv3](LICENSE). The license covers the code. It does not cover the XCommander name, the app icon or the xcommander.dev domain. These identify the official app, made by Wassim Mansouri, so users always know which build they're running.

## What you can do

- Build XCommander from source for yourself and keep the name and icon.
- Fork the code, change it and share your version under the GPLv3, under a different name and icon.
- Say that your fork is "based on XCommander" or "a fork of XCommander".
- Use the name to write about the project, review it or link to it.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a build, modified or not, under the name XCommander or with its icon.
- Using a name or icon close enough to be confused with XCommander.
- Suggesting that your fork, product or service is official or endorsed.

## Official builds

The only official builds of XCommander are published at [xcommander.dev](https://xcommander.dev), signed with Wassim Mansouri's Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you got XCommander anywhere else, it's not the official app.

For permission or questions, write to wassim@xcommander.dev.
